Concept B

A dedicated "Recoveries" lane on the Board, worked like a queue with a call script

Every mis-stock lands on a new lane next to the existing region cards, so support sees it the instant it fires — no separate screen. Cards move New → Contacting → Awaiting Consent → Resolved as the agent works the call script beside the same governed swap drawer (candidates, reason, price decision, acknowledgement, consent). Built for a support desk working several cases at once.

Seeded with 3 incidents distributed across the lanes. Click a card to open the case.
