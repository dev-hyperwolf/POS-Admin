Front-end · Weekly deal board

Calling out day-limited deals to shoppers

Most brands run all week, but some are live only certain days — Claybourne and Coldfire Wednesday only, Kanha Tuesday, CHKN N WAFFLEZ and Driftwood Friday–Sunday. The board currently shows only the discount, so a shopper can't tell. Five ways to surface it in Spaces — they stack, so pick one primary treatment plus optional urgency.

Recommendation
Ship A + B together

A Inline day tag is the baseline — it's the least disruptive and reads as the exception on an otherwise all-week board. B Layer the live-today urgency states on top so a Wednesday shopper sees "TODAY ONLY" glow and later drops read "STARTS FRI." That combination communicates the limit and converts on it, with no new browse UI.

Keep C filter chips in your pocket for when limited drops become a weekly rhythm (weekend flash deals) — that's when a day browser earns its complexity. Use D section split only for a campaign you're actively promoting as limited. E week dots are the most precise but the busiest — best reserved for a deal-detail page rather than the dense home board.
