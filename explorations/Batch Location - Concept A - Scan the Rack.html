Design mockup — every product, batch, quantity and rack location on this screen is example data, not a live inventory feed.
Every rack shelf carries a QR / RFID label — an address like A-03-2 (zone · rack · shelf), printed large and colored by zone. At receiving, the handheld proposes a slot, the clerk walks there, scans the label, reads the tagged units in bulk, and taps one button. A second batch of the same SKU is never proposed the same shelf as the first — it goes next to it, and the refill screen says so loudly. Every line on every screen below is one batch, never a SKU total — and every location is confirmed by scanning that rack's own QR/RFID label, never picked from a list.
