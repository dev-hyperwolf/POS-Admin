HR Overview — Concept B: People First

Track 3 · M1 read-only · reads GET /api/hr/employees, /compliance/expiring, /incidents, /calloffs
The idea

Overview is the roster. If HR opens this screen wanting to know "who," they should already be looking at rows of people, not a strip of numbers they have to click through.

The table is the screen: name, role, entity, store, doc status, last incident, last call-off — one row per person, filterable at the top. Everything the Day Board concept turns into cards (headline counts, the escalation queue, the role/PII explainer) becomes a right rail here instead: present, but secondary to the roster.

Every row comes from qa/fixtures/hr/employees.json joined against incidents.json/calloffs.json/Table A — the same join GET /api/hr/employees plus per-employee detail calls would do. Doc status is a computed flag (current/expiring/expired/missing), never the document itself. No SSN, DL, passport, address or emergency-contact field appears anywhere on this page — those never leave the server for this route, at any role.
