Model D — QR at the sign

Each curbside space has a physical numbered sign with its own QR code. The customer never taps "I'm here" or picks anything on screen — parking IN the space and scanning ITS sign is the whole arrival gesture. The code encodes store + space, signed, so a photo of someone else's sign can't be reused. Staff run a TV-style countdown board instead of a queue list.
