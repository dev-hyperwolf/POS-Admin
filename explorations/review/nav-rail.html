Nav Rail Review

Round 2. Four navigation kinds for the phone — drawer, launcher, category bar with a rising sheet, command sheet — each paired with its natural desktop model, and every mock has a populated Orders board behind the navigation.
What to look for

What changed from the rejected set: the first round's four phone views were all the same bottom-tab-bar + "More" sheet, and the desktop mocks sat on an empty placeholder page. This round: four different phone navigation models (not re-skins), four desktop models that pair with them, a real Orders board with 8 open orders behind every one, the store switcher and role chip inside the navigation surface, badge counts, favourites and recent, and search — in light and dark. Same 7 categories and 30 destinations (+2 Soon) in all four, per docs/NAV-RAIL-IA-2026-09-17.md.

  • Is each phone model different in kind? A is a drawer you pull from the left; B is a launcher home of tiles; C is a bar of category tabs whose active tab raises a half-sheet; D is a single floating "Go to" that opens a command sheet. If two still feel like the same thing re-skinned, say which two.
  • Who is each one best for? Drawer = someone who lives in 2–3 categories and wants the whole map one swipe away. Launcher = someone who starts from a number (open orders, verifications waiting) rather than a label. Category bar = a budtender or dispatcher who stays inside one category all shift. Command = a store lead or owner who knows the name of the screen and types three letters. Does the mock make that person's day faster?
  • Thumb reach. Where is the first tap? A: top-left hamburger (or an edge swipe). B: a tile in the middle of the screen. C: the bottom bar. D: a floating pill at the bottom centre. Every target is at least 44 px — check nothing feels cramped.
  • One-hand use. Can you get from Orders to Verify without a second hand? Count the taps and the stretches in each.
  • How deep are destinations? A: open drawer → expand category → tap (2–3). B: launcher → tile → tap (2, or 1 from a favourite). C: tap tab → tap active tab → tap (2, or 1 if the tab's last screen is the one you want). D: Go to → tap a row, or type and press Enter (1–2). Is the shallow path also the discoverable one?
  • How do the two "Soon" items read? LP Triage (Members) and HR Overview (People) are dashed, greyed and say "Soon"; tapping shows a toast, not a broken screen. Coming-not-broken, or clutter?
  • Store switcher and role. Lake Elsinore / Corona / Riverside and "Priya Nair · Store lead" appear in every mock — in the drawer head, the launcher greeting, the sheet's top bar, the command sheet's "For this store / For my role" sections, and in each desktop top bar. Does it read as one navigation surface, or as a chip bolted on (the problem IA §3.9 describes)?
  • Also: toggle Desktop / Mobile and Dark mode in each concept's own demo bar; use ↑↓, Enter, Esc, / or ⌘K; Tab through and confirm the focus ring is always visible. Find Verify and Members (CRM) — both have a home in every concept.
