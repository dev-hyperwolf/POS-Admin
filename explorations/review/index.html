Design review rounds

Six rounds, four concepts each. Open a round, enter the team PIN once, type your name, then leave keep / change / drop notes per concept and section. Everyone sees everyone's notes.

  1. Refill Day ReviewWhere the daily refill is planned and packed. The team already liked the kit column from B and the box panel from D.
  2. Batch Location ReviewWhere a batch lives on the racks and how put-away is recorded at receiving.
  3. Cycle Count ReviewCounting a location with the RFID handheld, variances, blind recounts and manager approval.
  4. Verify ReviewPack-verify at the bench, return-verify when the driver is back, and the paper-first path.
  5. Store ReviewIn-store flows: floor restock from the back, new arrivals, batch pick at the register, the store day.
  6. Build and Close ReviewThe weekly kit build, build review, day close, and quarantine / waste / recall.
  7. Form Builder ReviewThe manager screen for creating and editing a form — fields, types, showWhen, repeating groups, computed fields, photo/signature, publish.
