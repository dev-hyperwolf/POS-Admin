The idea

Start from the question a manager actually has, not a report name.

There is no free-text NLP here — every "question" is a fixed chip that maps 1:1 onto a report in wmdemo/reports.REPORTS, so what looks conversational is really just a friendlier picker over the same registry Concepts A/B/D use. Answer three quick chips (question, store, range) and the transcript shows the run, with follow-ups to narrow or export.

A question whose report needs reports:pii or whose export needs reports:export says so in the transcript rather than silently failing — same gate as every other concept, different voice.
