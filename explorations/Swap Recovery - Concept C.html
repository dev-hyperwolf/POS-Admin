Concept C

Driver, support and customer in one thread — the swap is a card in the conversation, not a separate screen

Every mis-stock opens a case thread: the driver's scan mismatch posts itself as a system message, support and the customer trade messages in the same view, and system messages narrate every governed step (kit check, consent, price decision) as they happen — so the whole recovery, including who said what, reads back as one continuous record. The swap itself renders as a card bubble with the same tabs and gates as the other concepts.
