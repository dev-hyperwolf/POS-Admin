Design mockup — every location, name, batch, quantity and timestamp on this screen is example data, not a live count.
Every session starts with an RFID read. Nothing adjusts stock until you approve.
Each row is one batch: a proposed read-vs-expected from the handheld, or — only for a dead tag or an untagged category — a flagged hand count. Approving posts the read (or hand-counted) quantity as the new on-hand for that batch at that location, and records who approved it; rejecting sends it back for a blind recount by a second, named person. No automated process ever writes an adjustment.
