The idea

One spreadsheet of every currently-effective rate. Click a row for its full history.

Every rate below is marked SAMPLE — this module ships with no seed data on purpose (docs/TAX.md); an ops admin confirms real numbers per store before WM_TAX_TABLE ever goes live. There is no in-place edit anywhere: "Change" always closes the open row and creates its replacement, and the confirmation always names the effective date and every affected store before either write happens.
