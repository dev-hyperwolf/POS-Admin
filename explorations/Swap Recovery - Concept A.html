Concept A

The mis-stock banner lives right on the order sheet the support agent already has open

The moment a scan mismatch or kit-ledger check flags an item as not in the driver's kit, a red banner appears at the top of that order's sheet — the same screen support already works from. "Open swap" slides in one governed drawer: three candidate ladders (Similar / Stronger / Same brand or price band), a reason, a case-by-case price decision that gates on a required "customer was told" acknowledgement, and a logged verbal consent. Nothing here invents a new destination — it is the existing order-detail screen, extended.

Seeded with 3 incidents. Pick one from the selector to see the banner, then click "Open swap" to work the case end to end.
