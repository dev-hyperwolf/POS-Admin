Merging a Weedmaps customer with our DB

A Weedmaps order can arrive matching someone we already know — or not. Three models for how the match becomes a merge, each with a different balance of speed vs. control. All three write the same audit trail into the customer's history in Members, so we always know what happened, why, and when. Option B is what's wired into the order panel today.

Option A · Auto
Auto-merge on high confidence
The system merges silently when the match is near-certain, and only routes the uncertain ones to a human. Highest throughput.
1WM order lands. We score identity on phone + email + ID + device.
2Score ≥ 95% → merge automatically, no prompt. History gets an "auto-merged" event.
3Score 70–95% → drop into a Review queue in Members for one-click confirm.
4Score < 70% → treated as a new customer; can be linked later.

Recommendation
Ship A + B together

Run A and B as one system: A (auto-merge) silently unifies the near-certain matches (≥95% on phone + email + ID) so staff never touch them, while B (always confirm) catches everything below that bar — every uncertain match surfaces the side-by-side compare in the order panel for a human to confirm or reject. One confidence threshold routes each WM order to the right path automatically: high-confidence → auto-merged, everything else → confirm. That combination is both fast and safe from day one, and it scales — as volume grows you only ever raise or lower the threshold. Keep C (link-not-merge) under the hood regardless — storing WM identity as a reversible alias makes every A/B decision safely undoable and keeps a perfect "which identity did this" trail.

All three share one contract: the customer's Members timeline records every match, merge, link and unlink with actor + reason + time. That history is non-negotiable — it's how we answer "what happened to this customer and why."
