# Hyperwolf Verify — tester guide (2026-09-15)

You have been sent a link that looks like `https://hyperwolf-wm-demo.onrender.com/verify/…`.
It is single-use and expires after an hour. Everything happens on your phone, in the browser.

## What you need
- Your **physical** driver's licence or state ID (any US state or Canada), **or** a passport.
  A photo of an ID on another screen will not work and will be declined.
- A bright spot without glare. Take the laminated card out of any sleeve.
- About 30 seconds. Nothing to install.

## What happens
1. **Terms** — one checkbox.
2. **Get ready** — pick *Driver's licence or state ID* or *Passport*, then tap **I'm ready**.
3. **Front of ID** — hold the card inside the box; it snaps by itself when it is sharp.
   Passport: open to the photo page, flat, with the two lines of code at the bottom inside the
   dashed band; it will not snap until it sees both lines.
4. **Back of ID** (licences only) — the barcode; it snaps the moment it reads.
5. **Selfie** — look at the camera and **blink** when asked. No buttons.
6. **Checking** — usually under 30 seconds. Keep the page open.

If a photo needs redoing you get a screen that says exactly what to fix and an **I'm ready** button;
nothing is lost, and you never redo a step that already passed. Reloading the page or locking the
phone is fine — it resumes where you were.

## Outcomes
- **You're verified** — done.
- **We couldn't verify this online** — the screen says why in plain words and points you to any
  Hyperwolf store with your ID. This is not a judgement on you; tell us what you saw.

## What we want from you
Reply with: your phone model, browser, ID type and state/country, whether it approved, and anything
that felt slow, confusing, or wrong (a screenshot helps). Passports from outside the US and
older/worn licences are especially useful.

## Privacy
Only Hyperwolf sees your photos. They are used to verify age and identity and to prevent fraud,
kept under Hyperwolf's Terms and Conditions (linked on the first screen), and never shared with
third parties — the whole check runs on Hyperwolf's own systems.
